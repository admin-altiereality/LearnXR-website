# Rajasthan Government Outreach Playbook

## Beachhead Strategy: Rajasthan First

Rajasthan is our primary market because:
1. **Existing relationships:** iStart incubation, state govt visibility
2. **Policy alignment:** Rajasthan IT Policy 2022 mandates digital literacy; NEP 2020 mandates experiential learning
3. **Budget availability:** RSLDC (₹500Cr+ annual budget), DoIT&C digital infrastructure allocation, Education Dept ICT budget
4. **Lower competition:** Few XR companies focus on state-level govt in Rajasthan vs. Delhi/Bangalore

## Target Departments (Priority Order)

### 1. RSLDC (Rajasthan Skill & Livelihoods Development Corporation) — P0
- **Use case:** VR-based skill training for ITI trades (electrician, plumber, fitter, welder)
- **Budget head:** Craft Instructor Training + ITI modernization
- **Decision chain:** MD RSLDC → Director (Skills) → Joint Director → Project Officer
- **Entry point:** iStart connection to Innovation Cell; seek introduction to Joint Director
- **Proposal hook:** "Reduce ITI training drops from 30% to <10% with VR simulation. Zero equipment damage costs."
- **Timeline:** 3 months from intro to pilot PO

### 2. DoIT&C (Dept of IT & Communication) — P1
- **Use case:** Digital literacy training for government employees via XR
- **Budget head:** Rajiv Gandhi Center for Advanced Technology / Digital Rajasthan Mission
- **Decision chain:** Secretary IT → Director (e-Governance) → Additional Director
- **Entry point:** iStart relationship; DoIT&C is aware of iStart companies
- **Proposal hook:** "Train 10,000 govt employees in digital skills with 40% faster completion via VR."

### 3. Education Department — P1
- **Use case:** NCERT-aligned VR lessons for govt schools (Class 6-12 Science + Math)
- **Budget head:** Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan (SSA) ICT component / RSCERT
- **Decision chain:** Education Secretary → Director Elementary/Secondary → DIET Principals
- **Entry point:** NEP 2020 implementation committee; seek introduction via education NGOs
- **Proposal hook:** "NEP 2020 mandates experiential learning. LearnXR delivers it at ₹200/student/year."

### 4. Rajasthan Police Academy — P1
- **Use case:** VR training simulations for cadets (crime scene, traffic, crowd control)
- **Budget head:** Police modernization fund / BPR&D grants
- **Decision chain:** DGP → ADG (Training) → Director, Police Academy
- **Entry point:** Innovation showcase; seek ADG meeting via Home Dept connection
- **Proposal hook:** "Reduce training time by 38%. Zero risk simulation for dangerous scenarios."

### 5. NHM Rajasthan (National Health Mission) — P2
- **Use case:** VR training for ASHA workers, ANMs, PHC staff
- **Budget head:** NHM training component
- **Decision chain:** Mission Director NHM → Additional Director (Training) → State Training Coordinator
- **Entry point:** Health dept innovation cell; WHO/UNICEF connection
- **Timeline:** Longer cycle (6-9 months). Start concept discussions in Q2.

## Procurement Channels

### GeM (Government e-Marketplace) — MANDATORY
- **Action:** Complete seller registration by April 15
- **Products to list:** LearnXR Platform License, XR Lab Setup, Custom Content Development
- **Why critical:** Many depts now mandate GeM procurement. Without listing, we're excluded.

### Rajasthan eProcurement (eproc.rajasthan.gov.in)
- **Action:** Monitor daily for relevant NITs (Notice Inviting Tender)
- **Keywords:** ICT in education, e-learning, digital training, skill development, simulation
- **Alert system:** Daily email digest + WhatsApp alert for matching tenders

### Direct Procurement (< ₹25L)
- **Action:** Most pilot-scale projects can be procured directly (single quotation below ₹25L)
- **Strategy:** Price pilots at ₹15-20L to stay within direct procurement limits. Avoid tender process for pilots.

### CPPP (Central Public Procurement Portal)
- **Action:** Monitor for central govt opportunities (NSDC, MoE, MeitY)
- **Timeline:** Y2+ for national-level tenders

## Outreach Sequence (Per Department)

### Week 1-2: Research
- [ ] Map full org chart of target department
- [ ] Identify budget heads and recent allocations
- [ ] Find mutual connections (iStart, industry, academic)
- [ ] Review recent tenders and contracts awarded
- [ ] Prepare department-specific one-pager

### Week 3-4: Introduction
- [ ] Request warm introduction via mutual contact
- [ ] Send formal introduction letter (on company letterhead)
- [ ] Follow up via direct call + WhatsApp
- [ ] Share relevant case study or demo video

### Week 5-6: First Meeting
- [ ] Schedule in-person meeting (always preferred for govt)
- [ ] Prepare customized demo (their use case, their data, their branding)
- [ ] Bring printed collateral: brochure, case study, compliance certificates
- [ ] Meeting agenda: understand their problems → present aligned solution → agree next steps
- [ ] Send minutes + thank you within 24 hours

### Week 7-8: Demo + Pilot Discussion
- [ ] Conduct live platform demo for wider team
- [ ] Provide trial access if appropriate
- [ ] Discuss pilot scope, timeline, success metrics
- [ ] Begin proposal drafting

### Week 9-12: Proposal + Negotiation
- [ ] Submit comprehensive proposal
- [ ] Address technical queries
- [ ] Negotiate terms within approved bands
- [ ] Get verbal confirmation
- [ ] Follow up for PO

## Relationship Maintenance Rules

1. **Monthly touch:** At minimum one value-add interaction per month with every warm contact
2. **Festival greetings:** Send professional greetings on Diwali, Holi, Republic/Independence Day
3. **Policy sharing:** When relevant policy updates happen, share analysis with contacts
4. **Event invitations:** Invite contacts to our webinars, demos, industry events
5. **Zero ask rule:** At least 3 value-add interactions before making any procurement ask
