# Government Strategy — Rajasthan First → National → Global

## Phase 1: Rajasthan Beachhead (Y1-Y2)

### Objective
Win 3 govt pilots in Rajasthan. Convert at least 1 into a state-wide deployment contract. Establish LearnXR as the default XR platform for Rajasthan govt training programs.

### Why Rajasthan First
1. **Home advantage:** Registered in Rajasthan, iStart incubated, local relationships
2. **Policy tailwinds:** Rajasthan IT Policy 2022, Digital Rajasthan Mission, NEP 2020 implementation
3. **Budget availability:** RSLDC ₹500Cr+ budget, DoIT&C digital infra allocation
4. **Lower competition:** No established XR player focused on Rajasthan state govt
5. **Replication potential:** Success in Rajasthan is replicable to 27 other states with similar structures

| Department | Use Case | ACV Target | Timeline | Champion to Find |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| RSLDC | ITI skill training in VR | ₹15-50L | Q1-Q2 | Joint Director (Skills) |
| DoIT&C | Digital literacy VR | ₹10-30L | Q2-Q3 | Director (e-Governance) |
| Education Dept | NCERT science VR lessons | ₹10-25L | Q2-Q3 | RSCERT Director |

### Tendering Strategy
- **Below ₹25L (direct procurement):** Price pilots to stay under this threshold. Avoids formal tendering. Target: RSLDC, Police Academy initial pilots.
- **GeM procurement:** Complete registration. Many depts mandate GeM for IT purchases.  
- **eProcurement tenders:** Monitor daily. Submit for every relevant tender — even if we're not perfectly matched. Build bid experience.
- **Rate contracts:** In Y2, pursue state IT rate contracts for ongoing deployment.

## Tender Tracking System

### Daily Monitoring Checklist
- [ ] eproc.rajasthan.gov.in — new NITs
- [ ] gem.gov.in — relevant product categories
- [ ] eprocure.gov.in (CPPP) — central tenders
- [ ] State procurement portals of target states (phase 2)
- [ ] NSDC/MSDE skill development tenders
- [ ] Global tenders (phase 3): UN, World Bank, ADB education projects

### Tender Response SOP
1. **Alert received** → GovRel evaluates fit within 4 hours
2. **Go/No-Go decision** → CBDO decides within 24 hours (criteria: fit score, competition, margin, strategic value)
3. **Bid team assignment** → GovRel (compliance), CBDO (commercial), CTO (technical), CFO (financials)
4. **Draft response** → 5-day turnaround for standard bids
5. **Internal review** → CEO + CFO review for strategic bids > ₹25L
6. **Submission** → 48 hours before deadline (no last-day submissions)
7. **Post-submission tracking** → weekly follow-up until decision

### Proposal Template Structure (Govt)
1. Cover letter (on letterhead)
2. Executive summary (1 page)
3. Company profile + credentials (2 pages)
4. Understanding of requirements (their language, not ours)
5. Proposed solution architecture (WebXR, no-headset, offline, vernacular)
6. Implementation plan (timeline, milestones, deliverables)
7. Team deployment plan
8. Past performance / case studies
9. Compliance certificates and declarations
10. Commercials (pricing breakdown, payment schedule)
11. Annexures (company docs, certifications, financial statements)
