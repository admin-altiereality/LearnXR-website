# LearnXR Marketing Playbook

## Positioning Statement

**For** government departments, schools, and students in India  
**Who** need effective, accessible training and education  
**LearnXR is** the only XR learning platform  
**That** works without headsets, without coding, without internet, in any Indian language  
**Unlike** Veative, ClassVR, or Eon Reality  
**Because** we're the only platform built ground-up for Indian government deployment at scale.

## Brand Narrative: "India's XR Education Infrastructure"

Not another EdTech app. Not VR for novelty. LearnXR is **infrastructure** — like broadband or smart classrooms — that India's education system adopts at government scale.

**Proof points:**
- ₹20L Meta XR Startup grant (global selection)
- iStart Rajasthan incubation (state govt recognition)
- NIDHI Prayas (₹8.5L DST grant — deep tech validation)
- FITT IIT Delhi Top XR Startup (academic credibility)

## Channel Strategy

### 1. LinkedIn — Founder-Led Growth (PRIMARY CHANNEL)
**Owner:** Growth Hacker (Kunal) + CEO (Gaurav)

**Strategy:**
- CEO posts 3x/week: vision, behind-the-scenes, pilot updates, thought leadership
- Co-founders post 1x/week each: domain expertise (tech, marketing, finance, business)
- Engage aggressively in EdTech, GovTech, XR comments and groups

**Content Pillars:**
1. **XR in Education** — why immersive learning works (research, data, examples)
2. **Building in India** — startup journey, grant wins, govt experiences
3. **EdTech Policy** — NEP 2020 analysis, state IT policies, budget analysis
4. **Product Updates** — demos, features, user stories
5. **Team & Culture** — hiring, values, day-in-life

**Content Formats:**
- Text posts (highest reach): personal stories, hot takes, thought threads
- Carousels: data-driven pieces, how-tos, comparisons
- Videos: product demos, user testimonials, event snippets
- Articles: deep dives, case studies, policy analysis

**Target:** CEO to 10K followers in 6 months. 50K in 18 months.

### 2. Case Study Engine (CRITICAL for Sales)
**Owner:** CMO (Princi)

**Process:**
1. Every pilot → case study within 30 days of completion
2. Format: Challenge → Solution → Deployment → Results (with numbers)
3. Design: professional PDF + web page + LinkedIn carousel
4. Distribution: sales deck insert, LinkedIn post, email to prospects, website
5. Languages: English + Hindi for govt audiences

**Template sections:**
- Organization profile
- Challenge they faced
- Why they chose LearnXR
- Deployment details (users, duration, content)
- Measured outcomes (time reduction, satisfaction scores, completion rates)
- Quote from decision-maker
- Before/after visual comparison

## Government Narrative Positioning

When speaking to government officers, the narrative shifts:

| Wrong Framing | Right Framing |
|---------------|---------------|
| "We're a VR startup" | "We provide XR education infrastructure for the state" |
| "Our platform is cool" | "Our platform reduces training time by 40% and is NEP 2020 compliant" |
| "VR headsets are the future" | "Our solution works on the tablets your department already has" |
| "We're an AI company" | "We enable your department to achieve digital literacy targets set by the policy" |
| "Sign up for our app" | "We deploy within your existing IT infrastructure with data stored in India" |
